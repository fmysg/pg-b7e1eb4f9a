City Marathon Draws Record Crowd

By Lena Fischer, 2026-10-12

More than 31,000 runners took part in Sunday's city marathon, the largest field in the race's 40-year history. Organisers closed six bridges for the morning and added two extra water stations after last year's heat.

The men's race was won in 2:08:41 and the women's race in 2:24:05. Volunteers handed out 90,000 cups of water.

The council said the event raised money for three local hospitals and that next year's race will start one hour earlier.
