Description

Our range of Building Maintenance Equipment provides market-leading performance and innovation. Including 18Vx2 Brushless, Compact Brushless and AC solutions, Makita BME has the products to keep you clean in any situation.

Cut the cord and not the power with our 18Vx2 range of power tools. Our 18Vx2 range offers more power, speed and run-time without the hassles of a cord. The best part is our 18Vx2 lineup is fully compatible with all LXT® batteries and chargers. Tough out bigger jobs with 18Vx2 tools and go back to work with any of our 200+ LXT® solutions.

Makita’s NEW 18Vx2 Brushless Upright Vacuum provides a genuine cordless solution to commercial carpet and hard floor vacuuming. The Brushless motor provides a max sealed suction of 12kPa and 30 minutes of continuous runtime on the Max suction setting. 4 stage brush height adjustment allows for optimum pressure of the brush for effective vacuuming of a range of surfaces.

  • 4 stage brush height adjustment to suit a range of floor types
  • Tool free brush change for easy maintenance
  • Brushless 18Vx2 technology to rival AC performance
  • 5L dust capacity with HEPA filter for 99.97% filtration
  • Removable hand wand for detail cleaning on the go

Detail Cleaning – Removeable hand wand and flexible hose for cleaning away from the vacuum

3 Stage Suction Control – Allows the user to achieve optimum runtime and suction power for their task

HEPA Filter – provides a high filtration rate of 99.97% at 0.3 micron

Quiet Mode – Operates at just 60dB on lowest speed setting to limit disturbance to nearby people

Easy Maintenance – Tool free brush change for easy maintenance on the go

On-board Storage – crevice nozzle and round brush are secured in the back of the vacuum for easy access

Specifications

  • Max sealed suction: 12kPa

  • Max air volume: 31L/s (1.9m³/min)

  • Continuous Runtime (Minutes) 2 x BL1860B (not included): Max 30

  • Suction power:

    • Max: 85W
    • High: 50W
    • Normal: 25W

  • Dust capacity: 5L

*Battery and charger not included
