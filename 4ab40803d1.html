These sunscreens for your face are great to wear all summer long

There are very few pieces of beauty wisdom that all the experts agree upon, but the importance of sun protection is a rare exception. Dermatologists are adamant that people should wear sunscreen every day, regardless of the season, to protect skin from harmful UV rays that can cause sunburn, premature aging and skin cancer. An SPF of 30 or higher is typically recommended for daily use and is especially important on your face because facial skin is more delicate, more frequently exposed to the sun, and more prone to visible sun damage and skin cancer.

In this article: Dr Dennis Gross All-Physical Ultimate Defense Sunscreen Broad Spectrum SPF 50, Kate Somerville Hydrakate Illuminating Sunscreen SPF 50+ Drops with Ectoin and Naked Sundays CabanaClear Water Gel Serum SPF50

The best sunscreen is the sunscreen you’ll actually wear, so make sure to use lotions that are comfortable, convenient, effective and work within your preferred skin care routine.
