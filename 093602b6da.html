Breakdown of Rates and Hours Needed to become a Private Pilot

You pay as you fly and after each flight, we bill your card on file. We also accept cash and check.

Everyone’s completion time is different and mostly depends on the individual’s study time and times per week flying. If you fly more often, you pick it up faster and typically save money in the long run. We recommend 2-3 times per week if possible.

FAA’s minimum flight time required for Private Pilot Cert is 40hrs. 30 of those 40 hours have to be with an instructor. You'll also have an estimated 30 hours pre/post flight and additional ground instruction. Therefore your minimum estimate is:

$800 for practical test
$175 for knowledge test
$275 for books and equipment.
