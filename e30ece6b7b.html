📌

Strava is great for tracking your workouts, but its built-in analytics can feel a bit limited if you're a data nerd (like me). That's why I built Statistics for Strava—a tool that digs deeper into your activity data to show trends, visualizations, and alternative stats that Strava doesn’t surface. Whether you're a weekend warrior or a hardcore athlete, you'll find new ways to explore your performance. You can run it on your own server, own your data, and even extend it if you like—perfect for devs and self-quantifiers who want more than the default dashboard. Key Features * Dashboard with various stats and charts * Detailed list of all your activities * Monthly stats with calendar view * Gear stats * Gear (component) maintenance tracking (instructions) * Eddington for biking and running activities * Detailed list of your segments and corresponding efforts * Heatmap * Strava Rewind, a fun way to look back on your year in motion * History of completed Strava challenges * History of activity photos * User badges
